About My Mentor

Dale Petroskey

CEO, Cenergistic

Former President & CEO, Dallas Regional Chamber

Former Asst. White House Press Secretary

Former President, National Baseball Hall of Fame

Dale Petroskey serves as the CEO of Cenergistic, the world's largest private energy conservation company, headquartered in Dallas, Texas. In the 40 years since it was founded in 1986 by Chairman Dr. William S. Spears, Cenergistic has saved organizations more than $7.3 billion, working with school districts, universities, hospital systems, commercial and industrial building owners, and museums in all 50 states.

Prior to starting this role on February 1, 2026, Mr. Petroskey led the Dallas Regional Chamber (DRC) for 12 years. Under his leadership, the DRC was named the #1 Chamber in the United States among 1,600 chambers. During his tenure, more than 300 companies moved their headquarters to the Dallas region, Dallas ISD became the most improved urban school district in America, and the DRC built strong relationships of trust with public officials while focusing on under-championed populations across the region.

His career also includes service as Assistant White House Press Secretary to President Ronald Reagan, Senior Vice President for Mission Programs at National Geographic, and President of the National Baseball Hall of Fame and Museum in Cooperstown, New York.

Mr. Petroskey serves on numerous boards, including The Alfalfa Club of Washington D.C., KERA public radio and TV, the Dallas Mavericks Advisory Council, and the Texas Rangers Baseball Foundation. In 2025, he was inducted into the Dallas Business Hall of Fame. He and his wife, Ann — both Michigan State University graduates — have three married children and four grandchildren.
