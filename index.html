About Me

An executive-track student, coached like an athlete.

I'm an aspiring business management executive with a deep passion for organizational development and the psychology of leadership. I'm driven by understanding how strategy, culture, and human behavior work together to build high-performing organizations and sustainable growth.

Through internships in management, marketing, research, and strategy, leadership roles in DECA, and founding student-led startups, I've developed hands-on experience in market research, operations, branding, and team collaboration. I care about community impact, and using leadership to create meaningful change.

About ISM

Independent Study & Mentorship

ISM is a highly competitive elective for juniors and seniors in Frisco ISD that lets motivated, high-achieving students deeply explore a career field of their choice. Through extensive industry research, professional interviews, and mentorship, students gain hands-on experience while developing professionalism, networking, public speaking, personal branding, and business etiquette. The program emphasizes original idea generation, product design, independent work, and goal setting — strengthening research, résumé development, and oral presentation skills that prepare students for both academic and professional success.
